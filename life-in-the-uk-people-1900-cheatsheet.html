Built from the Life in the UK reference text, transcripts, question inventory, and generated dashboards. Main scope: 20th/21st-century relevance, including earlier-born people whose named work, public role, death, or impact falls in 1900 onward. Older anchors are separated at the end so they do not distract from the modern list.
